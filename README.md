
# Memento (2000)

## A. Introduction

*Memento* is a mystery and thriller movie written and directed by Christopher Nolan. It was released in **2000** and stars Guy Pearce, Carrie-Anne Moss, and Joe Pantoliano. The movie follows a man who tries to solve his wife's murder while dealing with a condition that makes it difficult for him to form new memories.

## B. Synopsis

*Memento* tells the story of Leonard Shelby, a former insurance investigator who cannot create new short-term memories after an attack. He is trying to find the person responsible for his wife's death. Because he quickly forgets recent events, Leonard uses Polaroid photographs, handwritten notes, and tattoos to remember important clues. Along the way, he meets people such as Natalie and Teddy, but he is not always sure whom he can trust. The movie uses a non-linear story structure, allowing viewers to discover information as Leonard tries to understand what happened.

## C. Characters

1. **Leonard Shelby** – The main character who has trouble forming new memories and is searching for his wife's killer.
2. **Natalie** – A woman who becomes involved in Leonard's search and gives him information.
3. **Teddy** – A man who claims to be helping Leonard investigate the murder.
4. **Sammy Jankis** – A man whose memory condition becomes an important part of Leonard's memories and investigation.
5. **Jimmy Grantz** – A man connected to the events Leonard is trying to understand.

## D. Personal Review

This movie has been one of my favorites *Memento* reason being that its story is different from many other movies. It makes the audience think and pay attention to small details to understand what is happening. And unlike other movies that start the very beginning to further understand its plot, this movie starts at the ending making the start of the plot as its ending to further understand it. The movie flips the story which creates a very unique viewpoint as the audience.

### Three Things I Liked About the Movie

- **The unique storytelling** – The scenes are presented in an unusual order, which makes the story interesting.
- **The main character** – Leonard's memory condition makes his decisions and investigation challenging to follow.
- **The mystery and suspense** – The movie gives clues little by little, making viewers curious about the truth.

**My rating: 9/10**

I recommend *Memento* to people who enjoy mystery movies and stories that make them think. It is worth watching because the unusual storytelling encourages viewers to pay attention and form their own understanding of the events.
